# Shot list — *پرده‌ی آخر / The Final Curtain* — Scene 4 (sample)

> Sample storyboard demonstrating the new professional quality bar. Scope: `sample` (one scene, one page, 6 panels). Total runtime: ~24 seconds. Aspect: 16:9.

## Coverage strategy
Establishing → entry → recognition → insert (turning point) → reaction CU → crane-out for transition. The dramatic value flips from neutral observation to interior revelation on SH 4–5; SH 6 lifts us out of the scene and into the next.

## Director notes
- The turning point lands on **SH 4 (ECU)** — the letter's dust says: this was waiting a long time.
- **SH 5** is the value-shift CU: positive (curiosity) → negative (grief). Earned by the silence on SH 4.
- **SH 6** lifts us to the next scene without a hard cut; the crane-up plus the tar opens the transition.
- Light direction: key from upper-left, ~35° from horizontal (consistent across all panels for continuity).
- Eye-line: SH 5 looking down-right (into the paper); SH 2's pan ends with Maryam looking right toward the bench. Screen direction holds.
