## COVERAGE STRATEGY (notes for the cinematographer)

- **Grammar rule:** every section opens on landscape and closes on a face — animal, silhouette, or child. Maintain this through the rough cut.
- **Master shot per section:** lock the wide *first*; only earn CUs after the wide is in the cut.
- **Long-lens discipline:** use 200mm+ exactly twice — Section 4 (Tehran-Mashhad highway aerial, optional 200mm telephoto pass for compression), Section 7 (final wild family if drone permits; otherwise 35mm trap). The compression is the meaning. Do not overspend the lens.
- **Camera-trap is sacred:** use only as-shot; do not stabilise, do not crop in past 20%, do not colour-grade beyond noise reduction. The grain *is* the credibility.
- **Drone permissions:** Naybandan region drone shots require border-region clearance — budget time. Touran and Miandasht are easier; ICS can broker.
- **No-acacia rule:** every AI-generated landscape plate must be QC'd against the negative-prompt list. If a plate contains an acacia tree, a baobab, tall waving golden grass, or a zebra/lion in background, reject and regenerate.
- **Light windows:** all daytime shoots — 04:30–07:00 (blue + golden) or 17:00–19:30 (golden + dusk). Midday plateau light is unusable; budget around it.
