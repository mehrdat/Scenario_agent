## Primary Logline

> In a country most viewers have never associated with cheetahs, fewer than thirty Asiatic cheetahs remain alive — and their last hope hangs on six names the world was never told: Kooshki, Delbar, Iran, Koushki, Telma, and a cub called Pirouz who lived ten months and broke a nation's heart.

---

## Controlling Idea

A species does not disappear in silence; it disappears in anonymity. To name the last individuals is to refuse their erasure — and to make their survival the audience's responsibility.

## Genre

Conservation essay-documentary in the Attenborough-BBC tradition, structurally indebted to Asif Kapadia's archival-only method (no interviews; archive + camera-trap + narration), with a participatory outro in the Varda / Herzog mode (the filmmaker briefly steps into frame).
