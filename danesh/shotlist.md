> Scope: openings of all 7 sections + Pirouz death + the outro = 12 panels across 2 SVG pages.

Panels are labelled `SC<section>.SH<shot>`. Durations are estimated; the editor sets the cut.

### 1.1 — Cold-open aerial · Touran plateau, blue dawn

- **Light:** pre-sunrise blue, soft, motivated by horizon glow.
- **Sound:** wind only; distant sandgrouse; no music.

## COVERAGE STRATEGY (notes for the cinematographer)

- **Grammar rule:** every section opens on landscape and closes on a face — animal, silhouette, or child. Maintain this through the rough cut.
- **Master shot per section:** lock the wide *first*; only earn CUs after the wide is in the cut.
- **Long lens discipline:** use 200mm+ exactly twice — Section 3 (truck on highway, optional), Section 5 (cheetah walking away). The compression is the meaning. Do not overspend the lens.
- **Camera-trap is sacred:** use only as-shot; do not stabilize, do not crop in past 20%, do not colour-grade beyond noise reduction. The grain *is* the credibility.
- **Drone permissions:** Naybandan region drone shots require border-region clearance — budget time.

End of shot list.
