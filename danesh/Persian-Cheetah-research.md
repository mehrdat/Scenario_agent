## 1. The animal

- **Species / subspecies:** *Acinonyx jubatus venaticus* — the Asiatic (a.k.a. Iranian) cheetah. Genetically distinct from the four African subspecies; split estimated ~30–70 thousand years ago.
- **Status:** IUCN **Critically Endangered**. The only surviving free-ranging Asiatic cheetah population on Earth lives in **Iran**.
- **Population estimate:** Government and ICS surveys in 2022–2023 placed the wild population at **roughly 12 individuals** confirmed by camera-trap, with a possible ceiling near 20. In 2010 the estimate was ~70–100. The decline is precipitous.
- **Range:** Central Iranian Plateau — eastern semi-arid steppe, not African savannah. Key reserves:
  - **Touran Biosphere Reserve** (Semnan)
  - **Miandasht Wildlife Refuge** (North Khorasan)
  - **Naybandan Wildlife Refuge** (South Khorasan)
  - **Dareh Anjir / Bafgh** (Yazd)
  - **Khar Turan** complex
- **Morphology vs. African cousins:**
  - Slightly smaller, paler, sandier coat tuned to desert.
  - Thicker neck ruff for cold desert nights (Plateau winters drop below freezing).
  - Longer legs proportionally; built for short bursts across rocky steppe, not long sprints across grass.
  - Tear-stripes identical to African form.
- **Prey base:** Jebeer gazelle (*Gazella bennettii*), goitered gazelle (*Gazella subgutturosa*), wild sheep (urial), wild goat, hares. Prey collapse from poaching and livestock encroachment is the deepest driver of cheetah decline.

## 2. The landscape (critical for visuals)

The Persian cheetah habitat is **not** savannah. Get the look wrong and the film is dead on arrival.

| Feature | Persian Plateau (correct) | African savannah (wrong) |
|---|---|---|
| Substrate | Pale sand, gravel, salt flats, dark basalt | Red soil, dark loam |
| Grass | **Sparse, dry, ankle-high tufts** of *Artemisia*, *Stipa* | Tall waving grass |
| Trees | **Scattered Zygophyllum, Haloxylon (saxaul), tamarisk, pistachio**. No acacia. No baobab. | Acacia, baobab |
| Horizon | Dust-colored mountains, often snow-capped in distance | Flat or rolling green |
| Light | Hard, white, high-contrast; long blue dawn shadows | Warm gold |
| Other fauna | Gazelle, urial, Persian leopard, wolf, caracal, hyena, sand fox | Lions, zebras, elephants |
| Architecture trace | Caravanserai ruins, qanats, mud-brick villages | None / safari lodge |

Color palette per act: bone-white, ochre, terracotta, dusty blue mountain, charcoal basalt, single accent of cheetah cinnamon-sand.

## 3. The named cheetahs (case-studies the script references)

- **Kooshki (کوشکی)** — Male, captured as a cub in 2007 in Miandasht when his mother was killed by herders. Raised in semi-captivity at Pardisan Park, Tehran. Became the public face of the species; died 2018 of kidney failure, aged ~12. Father of Pirouz.
- **Delbar (دلبر)** — Female, brought into the captive breeding program at Touran. Mother of the 2022 litter that produced Pirouz.
- **Iran (ایران)** — Female, the first Asiatic cheetah to give birth in captivity (Touran, 1 May 2022). Three cubs. Two cubs died within two weeks (one from a lung deformity, one of unclear cause). The survivor was named Pirouz.
- **Pirouz (پیروز)** — "The Victorious." Surviving male cub of the 2022 litter. Hand-raised by Iranian Cheetah Society / DOE veterinarians after his mother could not feed him. Became a national symbol — featured on murals, Nowruz cards, social media. **Died 28 February 2023 of kidney failure** despite a heroic transfer to a specialist clinic in Tehran. His death was front-page news in Iran.

## 4. Why the population is collapsing

Five named pressures, in rough order of weight:

1. **Roadkill on Highway 81 (Abbas-Abad–Miandasht corridor)** and the Tehran–Mashhad highway. Multiple confirmed cheetah deaths from vehicle strike. Underpasses and fencing have been built but are insufficient.
2. **Prey collapse** — illegal hunting of gazelle and urial; livestock overgrazing pushing prey out of cheetah range.
3. **Livestock-guard dogs and shepherds** — direct killing of cheetahs caught near flocks. Documented loss of mothers leaving orphan cubs (Kooshki's origin story).
4. **Habitat fragmentation** — mining concessions inside reserves, new roads, ill-planned solar farms.
5. **International isolation** — sanctions have hobbled the captive-breeding program: import of veterinary equipment, GPS collars, and exchange with international zoos has stalled. The species cannot easily be supplemented from elsewhere because there is no elsewhere.

## 5. Conservation actors

- **Department of Environment (DOE / سازمان حفاظت محیط زیست)** — government agency, runs reserves and game-guards.
- **Iranian Cheetah Society (ICS / انجمن یوزپلنگ ایرانی)** — independent NGO founded 2001, runs camera-trap surveys, community education, monitoring. The most credible field-data source.
- **CACP (Conservation of Asiatic Cheetah Project)** — joint DOE / UNDP project, ran 2001–2018; replaced by domestic programs after international funding lapsed.
- **Pardisan Park (Tehran)** and **Touran captive-breeding center** — ex-situ facilities.

## 6. Cultural & historical layer (for opening / closing arcs)

- Asiatic cheetahs were trained as hunting animals by the Sassanid and later Safavid courts — the "yuz" of royal hunting miniatures. The Persian language preserves the word **یوز (yuz)** distinct from leopard (پلنگ).
- Akbar the Great of Mughal India reportedly kept ~1,000 trained cheetahs; many were imported from Persia.
- Until ~1950 the cheetah ranged from the Arabian Peninsula through Central Asia to India. The last Indian cheetah was shot in 1947. Iran is the last refuge.
- Pirouz's death (2023) triggered visible national mourning. The cheetah has become a contested national symbol — straddling environmental grief, government failure, and civic pride.

## 8. Why previous AI-video attempts failed (and how to fix)

Diffusion models trained on internet imagery have a **massive African savannah prior**. Any prompt containing "cheetah" defaults to:
- Acacia trees
- Tall waving golden grass
- Lions / zebras in background
- Maasai-style horizon

To override, every visual prompt must:
- **Lead** with location anchor — "Iranian central plateau, Touran Reserve" — before the word "cheetah".
- **Specify substrate** — "pale sand, gravel desert, basalt rocks, sparse Artemisia tussocks, ankle-high dry vegetation".
- **Specify backdrop** — "snow-dusted Alborz / Kopet Dag mountains in far distance" or "Yazd desert horizon, no trees".
- **Specify the animal** — "Asiatic cheetah, paler sandy coat, slightly smaller, thicker neck ruff".
- **Negative prompt every time**: `African savannah, savanna, Serengeti, Maasai Mara, tall grass, acacia tree, baobab, zebra, lion, elephant, giraffe, wildebeest, jungle, rainforest, green grassland, safari jeep`.

Where text-to-video still drifts, fall back to: generate a key-frame image (Flux / Ideogram / Bing-DALL·E) with strong location prompt, then use image-to-video on Kling / Runway with motion prompts only.

## 9. Production gotchas

- **Censorship & permits:** filming inside reserves requires DOE permits. ICS can broker. Drone flight near borders (e.g. Naybandan / Afghan border) needs separate clearance.
- **Heat & light:** Plateau midday is blown-out white; shoot dawn (4:30–7:00) and dusk (17:00–19:30). Night camera-trap footage from ICS archives is licensable.
- **Archive footage:** ICS, IRIB, DOE, and BBC Persian have historical Kooshki/Pirouz footage. Negotiate before script lock.
- **Music:** avoid clichéd "ethnic" santur loop. Better: minimalist string drone (cf. Jóhann Jóhannsson) with a single tar / kamancheh motif used sparingly. The desert wants silence.
- **Narrator voice:** ideal is a calm, restrained Farsi voice — not "documentary boom." Reference: Persian-language nature documentaries by IRIB's *Mostanad* channel, or a Farhad Mehrad-style gravitas.
